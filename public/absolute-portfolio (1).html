08Takeaways

Tone of Communication

A brand's vision has to be grounded in the reality of its audience. For Absolute, we found that optimism paired with honesty was the register that worked — it acknowledged the urgency without weaponising fear. Fear-mongering, we found, created distance rather than conviction.

Relatability

Complex scientific narratives land better when broken into emotionally legible fragments. Instead of explaining the science as-is, we translated it into experiences and stakes the audience already understood. Relatability doesn't dilute — it activates recall and investment.

Right Balance

Designing an interface for a mission-driven brand means holding two competing forces in tension: engagement and overload. The flow needed to feel seamless while still challenging the user to sit with difficult realities — without burdening them with cognitive load that breaks the experience.

01Investor Narrative & Positioning

A core part of the role was devising the narrative, content structure, and visualisations for investor decks — the documents that had to answer the hardest question Absolute faces: how do you explain a company that is simultaneously a pharma company, an agri company, and a materials company, all driven by the same underlying biological intelligence?

The challenge wasn't just communicating complexity — it was making that complexity feel like a feature, not a liability. Investors needed to understand that Absolute wasn't spread thin across three industries; it was operating with a single, proprietary scientific edge — the Nature's Intelligence Platform (NIP™) — that made it distinctively qualified to act across all three.

Narrative Architecture

Structuring the investor story from the macro — the planetary crisis — down to Absolute's specific scientific capability and commercial model. Every slide had to pull in one direction.

Content Structure

Mapping the logical sequence of claims: what the audience needs to believe first before they can believe the next thing. Building decks that don't just inform, but build conviction incrementally.

Visualisation

Translating dense scientific and commercial data into visual frameworks that could be absorbed in seconds — without dumbing down the underlying rigour.

02Brand Positioning Strategy

The central strategic question — how do we position a company that innovates across pharma, agriculture, and materials without sounding scattered? — is what much of the Founders Office work orbited around.

The answer we worked toward: Absolute is not three businesses. It is one scientific philosophy — nature as the world's most proven innovator — applied across three urgent domains. The brand needed to lead with the platform, not the products. NIP™ was the thread that stitched pharma, agri, and materials together into a coherent, investable identity.

The question

How do you stitch pharma, agri, and materials into a single, coherent brand story — without making it feel like three companies in a trench coat?

The answer

Lead with the platform. NIP™ is the innovation engine. The sectors are applications of the same biological intelligence — not separate businesses, but one capability in three expressions.

Positioning frame

Absolute as a nature-powered innovation company — not a biotech, not a conglomerate. A company that uses evolutionary intelligence as its primary R&D advantage.

The connective tissue

Every product, every project, every claim traces back to NIP™. The platform is the brand moat. Making that legible — across sectors, audiences, and formats — was the work.

03XPRIZE Competition — Shortlisted

I was part of the team that entered Absolute into the XPRIZE competition — and we were shortlisted from the first round.

My specific involvement centred on the carbon lifecycle calculations — working through the numbers that underpin Absolute's carbon sequestration claims, and collaborating with the right scientific and technical partners to validate and communicate them with rigour. Alongside this, the work involved research synthesis and impact storytelling: taking complex environmental data and translating it into a narrative strong enough to compete on a global stage.

Carbon Lifecycle Calculations

Working through the end-to-end carbon accounting — from biological capture to sequestration — and ensuring the numbers held up to scientific scrutiny at a competition level.

Partner Collaboration

Identifying and coordinating with the right external partners — scientific, technical, and institutional — to substantiate the submission and fill critical knowledge gaps.

Impact Storytelling

Translating raw research and environmental data into a compelling narrative — one that communicated both the rigour of the science and the scale of the potential impact.

04NIP™ & Absolute Planet 2040

At the heart of the day-to-day was a visionary long-term project: Absolute Planet 2040 — and the research infrastructure powering it, the Nature's Intelligence Platform (NIP™).

NIP™ is Absolute's proprietary database of microorganisms, biomolecules, and enzymes — built to be the largest of its kind in nature. The day-to-day research work involved mapping key projects against this platform: understanding what the science said, what the stories were, and how to communicate impact at both a technical and a public-facing level.

Himalayan Genome Project

Tracking genetic diversity and novel genomes across unexplored and extreme ecosystems in the Himalayan region — pushing the boundaries of what the NIP™ database holds and what biological intelligence we can learn from.

Revitalising the Ganga

Research and impact storytelling around restoring the ecological health of the Ganga River Basin — one of India's most vital and most pressured water systems. Communicating both the science of intervention and the human stakes of restoration.

Research & Impact Work

Ongoing synthesis of research materials across all active NIP™-powered projects — translating findings into impact narratives for internal strategy, external communication, and scientific credibility-building.
